BRO SQUAD

Top-down permadeath runs on islands bigger than the screen — the view scrolls with you. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse; your gun fires on its own, no ammo to manage. Your Bro trails behind you and auto-fires at nearby enemies. A dark blue border walls off the edge of the world, so you can't wander off by accident. Inside it, black chasm pits are real: step in and you fall, permanently. Orange lava drains HP the longer you stand in it — and enemies take both hazards too, if you lead them there. Clear every enemy to advance. HP and gold carry between levels; dying resets the whole run.

Inspired by Gun Bros (early iOS)
